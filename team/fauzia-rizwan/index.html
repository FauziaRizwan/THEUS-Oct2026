Profile

Urban planner and policy expert with two decades of practice across master planning, habitat planning, infrastructure services design and land-use policy in Pakistan. Director of The Urban Solutions Pvt. Ltd. and formerly with National Engineering Services Pakistan (NESPAK), where she led planning work on post-earthquake reconstruction, city master plans and national land-use policy.

Her portfolio spans mega-scale housing schemes for Defence Housing Authority, the Pakistan Air Force and private developers, alongside community-led habitat planning for the Aga Khan Agency for Habitat and water-sensitive urban design for WWF-Pakistan. She works with government, multilateral and academic institutions on regulatory reform, disaster risk reduction and sustainable urban design.

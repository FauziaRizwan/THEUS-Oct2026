Syed Rizwan Munir

Founder & CEO, The Urban Solutions

From Plan to Execution — building the standard for master-planned urban development in Pakistan.

With a relentless drive for excellence, Syed Rizwan Munir founded The Urban Solutions to bridge the gap between architectural vision and flawless execution. What began as a conviction that master planning deserved the same rigor as engineering has grown into a firm entrusted with some of Pakistan's most ambitious urban developments.

His strategic leadership has steered over 150 projects from concept to completion — each one a testament to precision planning, innovative design, and an unwavering commitment to client success. Rather than treating zoning, infrastructure, and habitat design as separate disciplines, Rizwan built The Urban Solutions around the belief that they succeed or fail together.

Under his direction, the firm has become a trusted name in urban development across the region — known as much for the plans it delivers as for the regulatory precision and feasibility-first thinking behind them. He continues to lead from the front on the firm's largest master-planning engagements, setting the standard the rest of the team builds on.

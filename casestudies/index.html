02
Climate-Resilient Regional Planning

DHA Quetta
12,500 Acres of Culturally-Adaptive, CPEC-Ready Urbanism.

Master planning and design for a mega-scale development bordering Quetta city. Urban Solutions authored the zoning plan, zoning regulations, and detailed layout for a habitat built around quality, safe housing and sustainable economic development — culturally adaptive, climate resilient, and water-conservative by design, while positioned to capture the potential of CPEC.

Water-Conservative by Design

City greywater is treated and recycled for irrigation within the habitat, easing pressure on Quetta's scarce groundwater resources.

Engineering-Led Master Plan

Geo-technical investigation, hydrological & hydraulic study, and site analysis underpin the water supply, sewerage, and drainage system designs.

CPEC-Positioned Growth

Zoning and regulations calibrated to seed sustainable economic development as the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor reshapes the region.

Technical Edge
Total Area 12,500 Acres
Consultancy Scope Zoning Plan, Master Planning & Layout Design
Engineering Scope Water Supply, Sewerage & Drainage Design
Design Philosophy Climate-Resilient & Water-Conservative Planning

Bespoke Boutique Living

Rahman Enclave
Where Strategic Connectivity Meets Elite Privacy.

Rahman Enclave is a testament to the "Boutique Community" model. Urban Solutions navigated complex CDA regulatory frameworks to deliver a Layout Plan (LOP) that maximizes land utility while maintaining an intimate, high-end aesthetic. It is a masterclass in urban infill and strategic land usage.

CDA Zone 4 Compliance

A legally seamless development designed within the rigorous standards of the Capital Development Authority.

Ecological Stewardship

Over 15% of the total land is dedicated to open green spaces and parks, significantly higher than standard regulatory requirements.

Integrated Connectivity

Engineered for immediate access to the GT Road, bridging the gap between Rawalpindi’s commercial pulse and Islamabad’s administrative heart.

Technical Edge
Project Type Premium Gated Community
Location Zone 4, GT Road, Islamabad
Regulatory Status CDA Approved (LOP & NOC)
Community Focus Pedestrian-Friendly Green Belts
